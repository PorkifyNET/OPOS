OPOS 0.6.8: the Browser now runs JavaScript, so many more websites work.

- The Browser runs the scripts on web pages. Menus, buttons, pop-ups and other parts of pages that are made with JavaScript now work, and pages that build themselves with scripts (such as BBC News and GitHub) now show their content.
- Sites can load more data while you look at them, remember your settings and log-ins (cookies and saved site storage), and change the address without loading a new page.
- Pages can draw with scripts (canvas), so charts, simple games and animated pictures on websites now show.
- Pages with modern scripts (modules, custom elements, observers) and styles changed by scripts now work.
- Sites you visit often open faster: the Browser keeps connections to a site open for the next request and keeps scripts it has already prepared.
- WebAssembly, WebGL and plug-ins are not supported yet.

Updating from 0.6.3 or older: VMware may hang at the restart at the end of the update. The update is already installed by then. Close VMware, end vmware-vmx.exe (taskkill /F /IM vmware-vmx.exe from an administrator command prompt; this stops every running VM) or restart Windows, then start the VM again.
