OPOS 0.6.1: a better OPOS Store.

- The OPOS Store has a start page with featured programs and categories, plus search: start typing anywhere in the Store to find a program by name, summary or category.
- Every program has an About page with its description, category, version, size, license, publisher, needed OPOS version and permissions.
- Store downloads and installs are about 4.5 times faster.
