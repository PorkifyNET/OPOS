OPOS 0.1.5: the OPOS Store, Linux programs and sound.

- New OPOS Store (Start > OPOS STORE): browse, install, update and remove signed `.opk` packages. Package updates also appear in Settings > UPDATES, and `/bin/pkg` does the same from the Terminal.
- Real Linux programs run on OPOS as packages: Lua, SQLite, BusyBox (shell, vi, less) and SDL 2 programs in their own windows. Dynamically linked musl programs work too.
- The Terminal is now a VT100 terminal with Ctrl+C, so full-screen Linux programs work in it.
- Doom runs on OPOS: Chocolate Doom with the free Freedoom game data, packaged as a Linux program.
- Sound: OPOS plays audio through Intel HD Audio and the Ensoniq AudioPCI 97 (VMware's default sound card). Programs can play sound, SDL programs and Doom included.
- Master volume: the keyboard's volume keys show a volume popup, and the level is remembered. `/bin/play` plays WAV files.
- Faster large disk writes.
