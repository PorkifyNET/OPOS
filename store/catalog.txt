# What the OPOS Store window shows of each package (tools/mkindex.py
# --catalog). One block per package id:
#
#   [id]
#   category: games | internet | office | graphics | sound-video |
#             programming | system | accessories
#   license: SPDX name or a short text (40 characters at most)
#   featured: yes          (optional: shown on the Store's start page)
#   official: yes          (optional: made by OPOS, listed under MADE BY
#                          OPOS; not for the SDK examples)
#   summary: one short line (90 characters at most)
#   description: the About page text, one line (900 characters at most)
#
# Every line is optional. Lines go into the signed index.

description: Greets you and shows your account, its home folder and the kernel ABI version. Hello is the smallest OPOS program and the first example of the OPOS SDK: read its source in sdk/examples/hello.c to see how an OPOS program opens a window and talks to the system.

[net.porkify.opos.minesweeper]
category: games
license: GPL-3.0
official: yes
summary: Clear the field without stepping on a mine.
description: Open every cell that holds no mine. A number tells how many of the eight cells around it hide one. Left click opens a cell, right click plants a flag, and a click on a number whose flags are all placed opens the cells around it. [net.porkify.opos.mediaplayer]

featured: yes
summary: Play music and videos: MP3, FLAC, OGG, WAV and MPEG-1 video.
description: Media Player plays your music and videos. Open MP3, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis and WAV songs or MPEG-1 videos (.mpg) and they go into a playlist: play, pause, skip, jump anywhere with the position bar, repeat one song or the whole list, or shuffle it. [net.porkify.opos.calculator]
category: accessories
license: GPL-3.0
official: yes
summary: Add, subtract, multiply and divide, or work with sines, logarithms and powers.
description: Calculator is the OPOS calculator. Type a whole calculation, like 12 * (3 + 4)^2, and see the answer while you type; press = to keep it in the history, and click an answer in the history to use it again. Standard mode has the four operations, brackets, percent, square roots and squares. Scientific mode adds sine, cosine and tangent in degrees or radians and their inverses, natural and base-10 logarithms, powers, cube roots, factorials, pi and e. Answers have 15 significant digits, so 0.1 + 0.2 is 0.3. Keys: digits, + - * / ^ ( ) % !, Enter for the answer, Backspace, Esc to clear, F9 to change the sign, Ctrl+C to copy the answer and Ctrl+V to paste.
